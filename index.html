Pokemon Champions team workspace

Build a clearer game plan before the match starts.

PokePilot is an unofficial workspace for building and checking Pokemon Champions teams. It keeps the useful parts of a team sheet, a damage calculator, and a second opinion in one place, so a change to a move, item, or effort-value spread can be checked in context.

Start here

Four ways to use PokePilot

  1. Start with a team or import a Showdown set. Choose a Pokemon, then set its ability, item, nature, moves, and effort values. Teams stay in your browser until you choose to save or share them.
  2. Check the shape of the team. The builder tracks defensive type overlap and missing attacking coverage while you edit. These are prompts for review, not rules that every team must satisfy.
  3. Test a concrete matchup. Use the calculator when a damage range, a speed order, terrain, weather, or a specific move interaction matters to the decision.
  4. Ask for a second opinion. PokePilot can summarize a team plan, examine a selected Pokemon, or suggest additions and sample adjustments. Read the result alongside the visible calculation evidence, then make the final call yourself.

What the app checks

Analysis is grounded in the team you built.

PokePilot does not treat a Pokemon as a list of isolated stats. Team analysis begins with the moves, abilities, items, and type coverage currently selected in the builder. Calculator-based evidence is kept separate from strategic suggestions so that a damage range never pretends to answer a team-building question on its own.

Team analysis

Reviews the team's likely game plan, role overlap, defensive pressure, and interactions that deserve attention before play.

Pokemon analysis

Looks at one selected Pokemon in the context of its teammates, rather than presenting a generic set without a reason to use it.

Recommendations

Suggests Pokemon or sample changes only after comparing the existing team. Availability depends on the current usage data for the regulation being analyzed.

Sample review

Compares viable natures, moves, items, and effort-value spreads without discarding the original role just to optimize one matchup.

Scope and sources

A fan tool with visible limits.

The builder currently follows Pokemon Champions Regulation M-C. It uses structured battle data derived from Pokemon Showdown, localized names and sprites from PokeAPI, and usage statistics where they are available. Newly released Pokemon can appear in the builder before public usage data is available, so usage-driven recommendations may remain on an earlier regulation until the data catches up.

AI analysis is optional. It can make mistakes, and its suggestions are not official battle advice or a substitute for checking game rules. PokePilot labels calculation evidence separately, preserves useful warnings when an analysis response is incomplete, and lets you inspect the underlying team state before applying a suggestion.
