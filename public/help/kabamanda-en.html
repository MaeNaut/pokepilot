Selection and game plan

Hippowdon + Salamence + a supporting member

This is a starting point when you have time to pressure the opponent with Yawn. Decide whether to establish Stealth Rock first or force an immediate sleep decision. An opponent with strong immediate damage or sleep prevention can interrupt that sequence.

The third member fills the missing role. Consider Primarina for Water/Fairy attacks and recovery, or Archaludon for an alternative hazard setter and physical support. With Meowscarada, plan how to avoid sacrificing Focus Sash to your own sand.

Choosing Mega Lucario Z instead

Compare the special route when physical checks prevent Salamence from breaking through even after Dragon Dance. Super-effective attacks against Dragon, Ice, or Steel types do not settle move order, priority, or Focus Sash. Lucario and Archaludon both have a Ground weakness, so do not fix them into every selection without support.

The goal is not to bring the three highest-usage Pokemon. Choose a route to a winning position, the turns needed to enable it, and an answer to what blocks it. Decide the Mega option, setup support, and finish together after examining the opposing six.

Weaknesses and adjustments

  • Focus Sash versus your own sand: consider using Meowscarada first or leaving Hippowdon out. If you consistently need both, compare another item rather than ignoring the conflict.
  • Duplicate hazards: the two Stealth Rock users are selection alternatives. Setting it twice does not stack its effect. When you always bring both setters, examine the opportunity cost of one move slot.
  • Salamence's HP: Stealth Rock, sand, and Double-Edge recoil can turn a survivable priority attack into a KO. Check whether a safe Roost turn exists.
  • Setup disruption: Taunt, sleep prevention, and fast immediate damage can stop Hippowdon's planned sequence. Do not automatically repeat Yawn without considering the opponent.
  • Conditional bulk: Stamina does not solve special attacks, and Aura Guard does not reduce every move. A type chart and an ability name alone cannot establish a safe switch.

Change one problematic matchup at a time. If Ice Shard knocked out Salamence, examine entry hazards, remaining HP, and Intimidate before changing its Attack investment. A defeat caused by prior chip is not automatically evidence that the offensive spread is wrong.
